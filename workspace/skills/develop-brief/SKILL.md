---
name: develop-brief
description: Develop a short explainer brief for review before drafting a storyboard.
---

Ask who the explainer serves and what readers should understand or do. Draft an
audience, one claim, the evidence supporting it, and a short outline. Label
assumptions. Ask the reviewer to correct or approve this version. After a
correction, return the revised brief for approval. Pass forward only the
approved version with its evidence and any production constraints.
